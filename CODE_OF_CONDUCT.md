# Code of conduct

This code of conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers, pledge to make taking part in Kleene
Star a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste,
colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Kleene Star is a project for learning. Many people who come here are
students meeting automata for the first time, and no question about the
subject is too basic to ask.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open,
welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being kind and patient, especially with people who are new to the
  subject or to open source
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback on code, designs
  and documentation
- Accepting responsibility for our mistakes, apologising to those affected
  and learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the people using it

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any
  kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email
  address, without their explicit permission
- Submitting someone else's work, such as coursework answers, as your own
  in a way that breaks their licence or your institution's rules
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Enforcement responsibilities

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards
and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any
behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, wiki
edits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code of
conduct, and will explain the reasons for moderation decisions when
appropriate.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies within all of the project's spaces (the
repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions) and when someone
officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

Report behaviour that breaks this code of conduct privately to the
maintainer, [Pratham71 on GitHub](https://github.com/Pratham71), or by
opening a private report through the repository's **Security** tab
("Report a vulnerability"), which only the maintainer can read. Please do
not report conduct problems in public issues.

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the
privacy and safety of the person reporting will be respected.

## Enforcement guidelines

The maintainer will follow these guidelines in deciding the consequences of
any action they judge to break this code of conduct:

1. **Correction.** For inappropriate language or other unprofessional or
   unwelcome behaviour: a private, written warning explaining why the
   behaviour was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning.** For a single serious incident or a series of actions: a
   warning with consequences for continued behaviour, and no interaction
   with the people involved for a set time. Breaking these terms may lead to
   a temporary or permanent ban.
3. **Temporary ban.** For a serious breach, including sustained
   inappropriate behaviour: a temporary ban from any interaction with the
   project. Breaking these terms may lead to a permanent ban.
4. **Permanent ban.** For a pattern of breaches, harassment of an
   individual, or aggression towards or disparagement of groups of people:
   a permanent ban from any interaction with the project.
